- **Deployed** — present on the production domain.

No item marked **Locally fixed** should be represented as deployed. No production deployment, DNS change, store submission, legal acceptance, or payment was performed during this audit.

## Material contradictions found

1. The live site’s actual production source was React/Next, while both nearby local website directories were Astro experiments. Vercel and GitHub resolved this in favor of `droidsize/droidsize.com`.
2. The live footer used the placeholder phone `+91 9999999999`; the company supplied `+91 99580 07011`.
3. The live site did not identify the legal entity, CIN, or registered office, despite being the organization’s official website.
4. The live homepage contained template awards, client logos, team profiles, testimonials, remote `randomuser.me` portraits, placeholder articles, and dead links without supplied evidence or publication approval.
5. The live privacy and terms content was template material, including an unrelated product name and placeholder text.
6. Product support and organization contact had been conflated. The remediation uses `contact@droidsize.com` for the company and `support@merrymagicmail.com` only for Merry Magic Mail.

## Current official requirement baseline

### Apple Developer and App Store Connect

- Apple organization enrollment requires the legal entity name, D‑U‑N‑S Number, authority to bind the organization, a work email on the organization’s domain, and a publicly available functional website associated with the organization.  
  Source: [Apple Developer Program enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/)
- App Store Connect requires a publicly accessible privacy policy URL. The policy and App Privacy answers must accurately describe the app and third parties’ data collection and use.  
  Source: [Manage app privacy](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/manage-app-privacy/)
- The App Store Support URL must lead to actual contact information so users can reach the developer about issues, feedback, and feature requests.  
  Source: [Platform version information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/platform-version-information)
- Apple’s review guidelines require a readily accessible way to contact the developer and detailed, accurate privacy disclosures.  
  Source: [App Review Guidelines, sections 1.5 and 5.1.1](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)

### Google Play

- Google Play organization accounts require organization identity, D‑U‑N‑S Number, organization phone and website, private account contacts, and public developer contact details. Google may display the verified legal name, legal address, public developer email, and public developer phone.  
  Source: [Required Play Console developer-account information](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312?hl=en)
- Organization accounts must verify website ownership through Search Console association.  
  Source: [Verify developer identity information](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10841920?hl=en)
- Each app must provide a valid support email; a website and additional support resources are recommended.  
  Source: [Support your app’s users](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/113477?hl=en-EN)
- Every published app must complete Data safety disclosures and provide a privacy policy, including apps that collect no user data.  
  Source: [Data safety requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en)
- If an app supports account creation, Google requires both an in-app deletion path and an external web resource where users can request account deletion.  
  Source: [Google Play Developer Program Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/17105854?hl=en&rd=4)

### India, accessibility, search, and security

- Section 12(3)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013 requires the company name, registered-office address, CIN, telephone/fax if any, email, and website if any on official business publications.  
  Source: [Companies Act, 2013, section 12](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/show-data?actid=AC_CEN_22_29_00008_201318_1517807327856&orderno=14&sectionId=195&sectionno=12)
- The website should prepare for transparent notice, rights handling, reasonable security, and grievance handling without claiming a privacy certification.  
  Sources: [MeitY DPDP Rules 2025](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa), [commencement notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf)
- WCAG 2.2 is the current W3C Recommendation and includes focus visibility, focus not obscured, and target-size requirements.  
  Sources: [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), [What’s new in WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/new-in-22/)
- Google recommends descriptive titles, canonical URLs, XML sitemaps, and accurate Organization structured data.  
  Sources: [title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link), [canonical URLs](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls), [sitemaps](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/overview), [Organization structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization)
- Security response headers add defense in depth.  
  Source: [OWASP Secure Headers](https://owasp.org/www-community/Security_Headers)

## Local remediation implemented

- Preserved the React/Next art direction instead of replacing it with the rejected Astro presentation.
- Rewrote the homepage around product strategy, interface design, engineering, and long-term product evolution without invented claims.
- Added permanent company, contact, Merry Magic Mail support, website privacy, and website terms routes.
- Added the legal name, CIN, registered office, organization contact Group, verified public phone, and named Grievance Officer.
- Kept product support separate from the organization contact.
- Removed template awards, clients, testimonials, team profiles, remote portraits, placeholder articles, dynamic content routes, fake project routes, and the unused dynamic Open Graph API.
- Added accurate route metadata, canonical URLs, Open Graph/Twitter data, Organization JSON-LD, sitemap, robots, and real not-found behavior.
- Added skip navigation, semantic landmarks, focus states, larger targets, responsive typography, reduced-motion handling, and mobile overflow safeguards.
- Replaced the oversized template navigation and footer with the supplied Droidsize logo, a compact responsive header, a keyboard-contained mobile menu, and a disclosure-led editorial footer.
- Added persistent warm-light and near-black Work themes with AA text contrast and a compact theme control.
- Removed the blocking preloader, global custom cursor, and smooth-scroll interception so the complete page is present before client JavaScript runs.
- Self-hosted the existing fonts and removed the old database/auth/content publishing dependency surface.
- Added defensive response headers and disabled framework disclosure.
- Upgraded to a maintained React 19 / Next 15 line and removed more than 900 unused packages from the lockfile.
- Added a dual-theme editorial Work directory, seven static project pages, verified public destinations, honest relationship/status labels, product-first copy, open-development notes, and icon-labelled technical stacks.
- Generated seven original project covers grounded in real product workflows and replaced generic capability imagery on the homepage with real Droidsize work.
- Added route-specific project metadata, project JSON-LD, sitemap entries, and a permanent `/works` to `/work` redirect.
- Kept the accessible letter-wise title reveal and broader motion pass deferred in a dedicated implementation plan until the static system is approved.
